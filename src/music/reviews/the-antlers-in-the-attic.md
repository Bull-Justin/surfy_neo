Probably the album I've come back to more than Peter's others. Not as heavy as something like Hospice but lordy I love 'In the Attic' and just how the album flows. 'The Universe...' was one of the things that helped verbalize a few of the things I was having issue with in therapy at the beginning. In an effort to not type a lot, I'll just say that if there was ever an album from The Antlers that I loved listening to beginning to end it would be this one.
